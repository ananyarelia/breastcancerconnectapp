# breastcancerconnectapp
Breast Cancer Connect is an Android application that helps New Jersey residents find breast cancer screening locations, understand insurance coverage, explore clinical trials, and access trusted healthcare information and support resources.

# 🎗️ BreastConnect NJ-01

**BreastConnect NJ-01** is an Android application developed for the **Congressional App Challenge** to make breast cancer screening information and support resources more accessible to New Jersey residents.

Finding screening centers, understanding insurance coverage, and accessing reliable healthcare information can be challenging. BreastConnect NJ-01 brings these essential resources together in one user-friendly application, helping individuals and families make informed healthcare decisions.

## Key Features

- **Find Screening Locations:** Browse breast screening and imaging facilities by New Jersey county, with direct access to Google Maps and phone dialing.
- **Know Your Coverage:** Understand screening mammograms, diagnostic mammograms, breast ultrasounds, breast MRIs, and general insurance coverage considerations.
- **Healthcare Policies:** Explore breast cancer screening policies, New Jersey insurance programs, patient rights, and public health initiatives.
- **Clinical Trials:** Search for breast cancer clinical trials using the public ClinicalTrials.gov API.
- **NJ Breast Cancer Statistics:** Access publicly available breast cancer statistics and information for New Jersey.
- **Support Resources:** Find support groups, financial assistance, counseling, educational materials, community resources, and helplines.

## Technology Stack

- **Language:** Kotlin
- **Platform:** Android
- **UI Framework:** Jetpack Compose
- **Architecture:** MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
- **Networking:** Retrofit and REST APIs
- **Data:** JSON and publicly available healthcare information
- **Development Environment:** Android Studio
- **Integrations:** ClinicalTrials.gov API, Google Maps, and Android phone intents
